02 / Published undergrad research

Road trip planner

I built this out of my own road-trip FOMO: what if I was right beside something great and never knew it was there? I wanted the route from point A to point B to surface restaurants and places worth seeing within five to ten miles — shaped by what a traveler actually cares about.

SignalI used Scrapy and Pandas to collect thousands of geo-tagged Instagram posts by hashtag, geocoded their location names, and applied ML text processing to filter for authentic posts.
ExperienceI turned the results into a Google Maps heat layer and marker layer. Red showed the most-frequented areas; opening a marker revealed the original post and caption.
Live routeA Raspberry Pi sent its location to ThingSpeak every few seconds. The map read that feed, traced the route to the destination, and followed the traveler in real time. I tested the prototype live with Hyderabad coordinates.
Built with: Raspberry Pi · Google Maps Platform · ThingSpeak · Scrapy · Pandas

05 / nemoSafe

Evaluating AI safety across the whole conversation

NVIDIA Spark Hack Series · Seattle · August 14–16, 2026
Do track · Active prototype; implementation is evolving
Targeted bounties: Best Use of NVIDIA Nemotron Lightning · Best Use of NVIDIA NemoClaw and OpenShell

A pre-release evaluation layer for AI products aimed at ages 14–17. Synthetic teen personas probe a frozen candidate across long, multi-turn conversations; per-turn and whole-trajectory evaluators identify and classify failures, producing evidence for a human release-readiness review.

Report design: safety, emotional intelligence, age appropriateness, and educational value. Safety is the first scored slice; the other dimensions remain under design.

Fixed tactic schedule→Response-conditioned simulation→Turn + trajectory review→Evidence report
71synthetic conversations
2,840candidate turns evaluated
42conversations flagged only at trajectory level
The product insight: Individually plausible replies can compound into secrecy, dependency, manipulation, or missed redirection. In the recorded fixed-arc run, 42 conversations passed every target turn under the stock NVIDIA classifier and were still flagged by the Lightning trajectory judge.

Built locally around Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, Nemotron Content Safety, a frozen Qwen candidate, and NVIDIA GB10 hardware, with NemoClaw and OpenShell as the target orchestration and containment layers. A planned live safety sentinel will support controller decisions and hard stops—not supply the final score. The current prototype uses a fixed tactic schedule; response-aware tactic selection remains deferred. Findings use synthetic conversations and are not a certification or a substitute for expert review.
