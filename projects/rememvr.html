03 / RememVR · Cognitive health

Making cognitive testingmore approachable.

A memory-aid concept pairing smartwatch and VR experiences. The work explored how a gamified experience and a clinician-facing view could reduce friction in cognitive assessment.

The challenge

Assessment has friction on both sides of the table.

I interviewed users, doctors, and caregivers to understand where cognitive assessment breaks down. RememVR asked two questions: could a gamified experience make testing more approachable for the person being assessed, and could a clinician-facing view shorten the diagnosis period?

What I did

Research through to a connected prototype.

  1. Interviewed users, doctors, and caregivers to identify pain points.
  2. Designed a gamified VR cognitive-testing experience paired with a smartwatch.
  3. Built a Flask/Python clinician dashboard to help shorten the diagnosis period.
  4. Connected the pieces into a working prototype as the full-stack IoT developer.
