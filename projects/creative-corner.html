The idea

Support for busy parents, disguised as play.

Creative Corner turns learning into interactive challenges and story-led play, with recommendations personalized to each child, so a parent with little time still has a way to be part of their child’s learning.

What we built

Three working pieces in one weekend.

  1. An image-recognition feature for interactive challenges.
  2. An example game storyline to show how story-led play would feel.
  3. A children’s book recommender for personalized suggestions.
