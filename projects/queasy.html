The idea

A research-backed fix, built for everyday passengers.

quEASY is a tilting mobile interface designed to reduce motion sickness in the car. The aim was an intervention grounded in research that an ordinary passenger could realistically use, not a result that only works in the lab.

What I did

Design, hardware, and testing.

  1. Designed the product: a tilting mobile interface aimed at reducing car motion sickness.
  2. Built the connected prototype as the project’s IoT developer.
  3. Ran usability testing with the working prototype.
  4. Validated the consumer feasibility of a research-backed solution.
  5. Published and presented the work at PETRA 2022 and IEEE GHTC 2022.
