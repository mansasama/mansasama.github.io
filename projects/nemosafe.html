A pre-release evaluation layer for AI products aimed at ages 14–17. Synthetic teen personas probe a frozen candidate across long, multi-turn conversations; per-turn and whole-trajectory evaluators identify and classify failures, producing evidence for a human release-readiness review.

The insight

Plausible replies can add up to real harm.

Individually plausible replies can compound into secrecy, dependency, manipulation, or missed redirection. A turn-by-turn check can pass every message and still miss the shape of the whole conversation, and that risk matters most for products built for teenagers.

How it works

Four stages, one evidence trail.

01Fixed tactic schedule

Synthetic teen personas follow a planned arc of tactics

02Response-conditioned simulation

Long, multi-turn conversations with a frozen candidate

03Turn + trajectory review

Per-turn and whole-conversation evaluators classify failures

04Evidence report

Input to a human release-readiness review

Results

What the recorded run showed.

71synthetic conversations
2,840candidate turns evaluated
42conversations flagged only at trajectory level

In the recorded fixed-arc run, 42 conversations passed every target turn under the stock NVIDIA classifier and were still flagged by the Lightning trajectory judge.

Report design

Four dimensions, one scored so far.

The report covers safety, emotional intelligence, age appropriateness, and educational value. Safety is the first scored slice; the other dimensions are still being designed.

Status & limits

An honest read on where it stands.

Built locally around Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, Nemotron Content Safety, a frozen Qwen candidate, and NVIDIA GB10 hardware, with NemoClaw and OpenShell as the target orchestration and containment layers. A live safety sentinel was planned to support controller decisions and hard stops, not supply the final score. The hackathon prototype uses a fixed tactic schedule; response-aware tactic selection was left for future work.

Targeted bounties: Best Use of NVIDIA Nemotron Lightning · Best Use of NVIDIA NemoClaw and OpenShell.

Findings use synthetic conversations and are not a certification or a substitute for expert review.
